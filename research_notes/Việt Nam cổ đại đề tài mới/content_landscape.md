# Vietnamese short-form content on ancient Vietnamese history: what is saturated, what is underserved, and which formats work (2024–2026)

Research date: 2026-11-17. About 17 tool calls were made. **Caveat on method:** I could not browse TikTok or YouTube search results directly, so no live view counts were collected. All view and follower figures below come from Vietnamese press and are as of the article dates. Treat them as approximate, and note that they were not independently verified.

## 1. Popular ancient-history topics and channels on Vietnamese TikTok, YouTube and Facebook

### Takeaway
The best-known Vietnamese history channels (Dòng Máu Việt, EZ Sử, Sử lược, Sử Việt oai hùng, Người kể sử Việt, Hồn thiêng sử Việt) focus on a few kinds of content:
- "4,000 years" overview summaries
- Kings and national heroes
- Great battles and changes in territory
- Patriotic, heroic framing

This makes the familiar canon heavily covered: Hùng Vương and Thánh Gióng, Hai Bà Trưng, Ngô Quyền at Bạch Đằng, Đinh Bộ Lĩnh, the Trần–Mông Nguyên wars, and Lê Lợi. In 2025–2026 AI-generated cinematic and "xuyên không" (time-travel) formats have entered the field.

### Cited Findings
- **Dòng Máu Việt** (Phạm Huy Hoàng, Hà Nội, started 2021): 1.35M followers, which the article implies is the YouTube figure, plus 750M+ total views, 2.5M TikTok followers and 100K+ on Facebook. Its topics are the contributions of Vietnamese heroes and inspiring, patriotic stories. Videos are under 1 minute and made on a phone and an old computer. The creator checks facts against *Đại Việt sử ký toàn thư*. The channel was locked several times, once permanently, for posting war imagery. — [Tiền Phong, 03/09/2024](https://tienphong.vn/nhung-nguoi-tre-lam-clip-lich-su-trieu-view-post1668069.tpo)
- **EZ Sử** (Đoàn Hồng Nam plus a team of 9, started early 2021): 424K+ YouTube subscribers, 65M+ total views and 100+ videos. It uses motion graphics with a soft voiceover. Its flagship "Tóm tắt nhanh Lịch sử Việt Nam qua 4.000 năm" runs 21 minutes and has 13M views and 173K likes. Topics are summaries of territory across periods, kings, national heroes and big battles. Short clips are reposted to TikTok and Facebook. — [Tiền Phong, 03/09/2024](https://tienphong.vn/nhung-nguoi-tre-lam-clip-lich-su-trieu-view-post1668069.tpo)
- Other channels named in the press:
  - YouTube: Dòng máu Việt, Sử lược - Tóm tắt lịch sử and EZ - Sử, each with "hundreds of thousands to millions" of followers.
  - 2D animation: Miền cổ tích (128K+ subscribers) and Vạn tích (45K+). Example titles are "Đinh Bộ Lĩnh dẹp loạn 12 sứ quân" and "Giai thoại lịch sử Hai Bà Trưng".
  - TikTok: Sử Việt oai hùng and Người kể sử Việt. The article says most of their clips reach millions of views but gives no specific numbers.
  - Recurring topics across these channels: periods of history, borders, kings and national heroes, big battles, dynastic issues and anecdotes about historical figures.
  - Source: [Đại Đoàn Kết, 09/09/2024](https://daidoanket.vn/ke-chuyen-lich-su-thoi-4-0-10289686.html)
- **AI-generated history on TikTok:** The channel "Hồn thiêng lịch sử / Hồn thiêng sử Việt" (@hon_thieng_su_viet, Phạm Bá Việt) has nearly 330K followers. The article estimates its cumulative views at "cả trăm triệu" (hundreds of millions), with no source given.
  - Formats: role-play, xuyên không (time travel), re-enacting events from a modern perspective, and AI recreations of figures, settings and narration.
  - The article names no specific eras or figures and is promotional in tone.
  - Source: [Sở hữu Trí tuệ & Sáng tạo, 31/03/2026](https://sohuutritue.net.vn/nguoi-tre-hoi-sinh-lich-su-viet-nam-tren-tiktok-bang-ai-d371343.html)
- A TikTok account, morphic.studio, posted "TRUYỀN THUYẾT THÁNH GIÓNG (Bản Cinematic AI)". An aggregator showed about 20.1K interactions, and it is unclear whether that means views or likes. This is unverified and comes from the search-result summary only. — [TikTok discover page](https://www.tiktok.com/discover/thuy%E1%BA%BFt-tr%C3%ACnh-v%E1%BB%81-truy%E1%BB%81n-thuy%E1%BA%BFt-th%C3%A1nh-gi%C3%B3ng-b%E1%BA%B1ng-video)
- Older YouTube examples of saturated topics include "Khởi nghĩa Hai Bà Trưng - Cuộc chiến chống xâm lược của nhà Hán [Lịch sử Việt Nam P5]" and multiple other Hai Bà Trưng videos. — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F4pxLgh4Qqs); [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FC5Rf5daGz0)
- **Global AI "POV: You wake up in…" trend:** The account @timetravellerpov led the trend. "POV: You Wake Up in 1351 During the Black Plague" reportedly drew about 19M views. The format puts a fictional narrator into a real historical event in a modern vlog style, using AI visuals and narration. Criticism includes AI artifacts such as extra fingers and a disrespectful treatment of real tragedies. - **Chinese reference point for AI historical drama scale:**
  - VnExpress reported a Chinese AI-made drama set in the Song dynasty with about 6 billion views. Its AI visuals, with stiff faces and unpolished scenes, were its most criticized element. — [VnExpress](https://vnexpress.net/phim-ai-boi-canh-thoi-tong-gay-sot-voi-6-ty-luot-xem-5141207.html)
  - The AI short "Huo Qubing" was reportedly made for about 3,000 yuan. — [iTiger](https://www.itiger.com/news/1138336589)

### Inferences
- The saturated zone is the heroic and military canon: Hùng Vương and Thánh Gióng legends, Hai Bà Trưng, Ngô Quyền and Bạch Đằng 938, Đinh Bộ Lĩnh and the 12 sứ quân, the Trần–Mông Nguyên wars, and Lê Lợi. "4,000-year summary" overviews are also heavily covered. Every named channel uses some version of this.
- Viewers in Vietnam already know the global "POV: you wake up in…" and "xuyên không" formats. Hồn thiêng sử Việt uses xuyên không role-play. Vietnamese-specific versions such as "nếu bạn sống ở thời Văn Lang" or "POV bạn là dân Giao Chỉ" did not show up in press coverage. This suggests the format is used but not yet saturated for the pre-938 period. This is unverified.
- **"Camera giám sát thời xưa" (CCTV of ancient times):** I found no Vietnamese press coverage of this trend. Searches returned only articles about modern AI cameras. Its prevalence on Vietnamese TikTok is unknown.

### Gaps
- No live TikTok or YouTube search data, no hashtag view totals (#lichsuvietnam, #povlichsu and others) and no current 2026 follower counts.
- No per-topic breakdown of which ancient figures get the most views.
- No Vietnamese-language source confirmed the size of the "nếu bạn sống ở thời…" or CCTV-of-ancient-times trends in Vietnam.

## 2. Pre-938 topics that appear rarely

### Takeaway
I found no direct evidence that anyone has measured how much each topic is covered. However, searches for Sĩ Nhiếp, Khúc Thừa Dụ and Mai Thúc Loan returned no Vietnamese short-video results, only Wikipedia and unrelated pages. In press profiles of top channels, the Bắc thuộc millennium shows up only through Hai Bà Trưng and the 938 endpoint. This suggests the middle of the Bắc thuộc period and the daily life of the Văn Lang–Âu Lạc era are underserved. That is an inference from absence, not a measurement.

### Cited Findings
- A combined search for "Sĩ Nhiếp", "Khúc Thừa Dụ" and "Mai Thúc Loan" plus "video lịch sử" returned no Vietnamese video or channel results. The only relevant hit was the English Wikipedia page on Mai Thúc Loan. It says he came from Mai Phụ village (Thạch Hà) and led a 722 uprising that the Tang quickly crushed. *Đại Việt sử ký toàn thư* treated him as a rebel against Tang authority, while modern national historiography treats him as a Vietnamese leader. — [Wikipedia: Mai Thúc Loan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mai_Th%C3%BAc_Loan)
- The press lists the topics of the leading channels as kings, national heroes, big battles, territory and dynastic issues. It does not mention daily life, material culture (Đông Sơn, betel, tooth blackening, tattooing) or figures from the middle of the Bắc thuộc period. — [Đại Đoàn Kết, 09/09/2024](https://daidoanket.vn/ke-chuyen-lich-su-thoi-4-0-10289686.html); [Tiền Phong, 03/09/2024](https://tienphong.vn/nhung-nguoi-tre-lam-clip-lich-su-trieu-view-post1668069.tpo)
- Even a carefully produced 2D animated history project centred its work on research into textbooks, ancient statues and paintings for costume and weapons. This shows that visual reference for the ancient period is scarce and costly to get right. — [Dân trí, 20/05/2025](https://dantri.com.vn/cong-nghe/6-tieng-cho-1-giay-len-hinh-trong-phim-hoat-hinh-lam-song-lai-su-viet-20250520122349157.htm)

### Inferences
- Likely underserved pre-938 topics, inferred from absence in press and search results:
  - Sĩ Nhiếp, who is controversial: "Sĩ Vương" in old chronicles versus a Han official in modern views
  - Mã Viện and the "xe ý dĩ" (Job's tears) story
  - Lý Bí and Vạn Xuân, beyond a brief mention
  - Mai Thúc Loan (722) and Phùng Hưng (Bố Cái Đại Vương, 8th century)
  - The Khúc family (Khúc Thừa Dụ, Khúc Hạo's reforms) and Dương Đình Nghệ
  - Đông Sơn daily life: bronze drums, houses on stilts, betel chewing, tooth blackening, tattooing, rice farming
  - The Âu Lạc and Cổ Loa crossbow legend told as social history
- Several of these figures are politically or historiographically ambiguous. Mai Thúc Loan was a rebel in old chronicles and is a national hero today. Sĩ Nhiếp was honoured in old chronicles and is viewed critically today. That ambiguity may explain why mainstream patriotic channels avoid them. It also means creators need careful framing.

### Gaps
- No quantitative coverage counts (number of videos or views per figure) for any of these topics.
- I did not run separate searches for Phùng Hưng, Dương Đình Nghệ, Lý Bí, Đông Sơn daily life or tooth blackening and betel, so their rarity is inferred rather than checked.

## 3. Controversies in Vietnam (2024–2026) over AI-made or inaccurate history videos, and what media and authorities say

### Takeaway
The most important case is the MV "Kiếp sau vẫn là người Việt Nam" (August 2025). Viewer backlash over AI-generated historical and national-symbol errors forced the producers to hide the video within days. Vietnamese press regularly warns that AI history content invents dialogue, mixes costumes from different dynasties and misplaces symbols. Authorities frame "xuyên tạc lịch sử" (distorting history) as a cyberspace-management issue under Decree 147/2024. Vietnam's AI Law has been in force since 1 March 2026 and adds labelling duties for AI content that simulates real people or real events.

### Cited Findings
- **MV "Kiếp sau vẫn là người Việt Nam"** (VnExpress, 08/08/2025):
  - Errors cited:
    - An AI scene of Trần Quốc Toản shows hills, but the 1282 Bình Than meeting took place in lowland Trần Xá.
    - Hoàng Sa and Trường Sa were shown inaccurately on a map.
    - Bronze drum patterns were distorted.
    - A rooftop national flag did not meet the official standard.
    - An extra hand appeared in one scene, and AI performers had stiff faces.
  - The producer said the team had "ghi nhận và rút kinh nghiệm" (acknowledged the feedback and learned from it). The team said it aimed to suggest history through metaphor, not recreate it.
  - On the evening of 8 August 2025 the MV was hidden on YouTube and replaced with an audio-only version.
  - Source: [VnExpress, 08/08/2025](https://vnexpress.net/mv-kiep-sau-van-la-nguoi-viet-nam-bi-nhan-xet-lam-dung-ai-4924481.html)
  - Also covered by [VTC News](https://vtcnews.vn/mv-kiep-sau-van-la-nguoi-viet-nam-gay-tranh-cai-lam-dung-ai-sai-lech-lich-su-ar958741.html), [PLO](https://plo.vn/mv-kiep-sau-van-la-nguoi-viet-nam-va-cu-viet-vi-lam-dung-ai-post864779.html), [Tuổi Trẻ](https://tuoitre.vn/phunuonline/mv-su-dung-ai-bi-phan-ung-e-kip-noi-san-sang-lang-nghe-sua-chua-1101557119.htm), [Mực Tím/Tuổi Trẻ](https://muctim.tuoitre.vn/kiep-sau-van-la-nguoi-viet-nam-co-lam-sai-lech-van-hoa-lich-su-101250808165855401.htm) and [Báo Quảng Ninh](https://baoquangninh.vn/mv-kiep-sau-van-la-nguoi-viet-nam-bi-chi-trich-dung-ai-lam-sai-lech-nhieu-chi-tiet-lich-su-post3370709.html).
  - **Conflict:** A search-engine summary said the MV's scenes of Hai Bà Trưng and Ngô Quyền also drew accuracy doubts. The VnExpress article I fetched does not mention them, so that claim is unverified.
- **Báo Gia Lai, 02/09/2026, "Đừng để AI 'kể sai' lịch sử, văn hóa":**
  - In an AI video about the Jrai hero A Sanh, the costume switches between brocade and a southern áo bà ba with khăn rằn. His dugout canoe becomes a southern xuồng ba lá. A dragon motif appears, though dragons are not a Central Highlands symbol.
  - An African baobab tree appears in a Central Highlands forest.
  - Dr Lưu Hồng Sơn (Pleiku Museum): "Nếu người thực hiện không có tri thức, nền tảng vững chắc về lịch sử, văn hóa vùng, sẽ bị AI dẫn dắt" ("Without solid knowledge of regional history and culture, the creator will be led by the AI"). He recommends cross-checking sources and having specialists review videos before posting.
  - Source: [Báo Gia Lai](https://baogialai.com.vn/dung-de-ai-ke-sai-lich-su-van-hoa-post596316.html)

It warns that a wrong ceremonial costume, a misused symbol or an unsourced quote creates false understanding. A person in Đà Nẵng was fined 7.5M VND for posting an AI-edited mascot image with false information. These points come from search-engine summaries of [Văn hóa & Phát triển](https://vanhoavaphattrien.vn/su-that-lich-su-truoc-lan-song-ai-a34156.html) and [Lao động Đoàn thể](https://laodongdoanthe.vn/lam-chu-tri-tue-nhan-tao-phat-trien-cong-nghe-giu-gin-ban-sac-ky-2-ban-quyen-du-lieu-va-ban-sac-van-hoa-viet-truoc-lan-song-ai-tao-sinh-118848.html); I did not fetch either article.

### Inferences
- The main sensitivities for AI ancient-history content in Vietnam are:
  1. National symbols: bronze drum patterns, flags, maps and especially Hoàng Sa and Trường Sa.
  2. Costume and setting anachronisms, and mixing regional or dynastic styles. Chinese-looking Han or Tang costumes on Vietnamese figures are a likely flashpoint for pre-938 content.
  3. Invented quotes attributed to heroes.
  4. Topics on sovereignty and the Bắc thuộc period, which the state frames as ideologically sensitive.
- Backlash can force a takedown within about 24–48 hours.
- Since March 2026, AI recreations of real historical figures and events published in Vietnam probably need a visible "AI-generated" label under domestic law, as well as under platform rules.

### Gaps
- I found no specific case of authorities fining or removing a video about an ancient national hero, such as Hai Bà Trưng or Ngô Quyền, for distortion.
- The exact sanctions for unlabelled AI content under the 2026 decrees are unclear because the sources conflict.
- The full text of the AI Law's labelling article was not retrieved.

## 4. TikTok and YouTube requirements for labelling realistic AI content (2025–2026)

### Takeaway
Both platforms require disclosure of realistic AI-generated content. YouTube's Help Center explicitly covers "a realistic scene that didn't occur" and making real people appear to say or do things they didn't. Fully animated or clearly non-realistic content is exempt. TikTok requires labels for AI-generated content (AIGC) showing realistic people or scenes and auto-labels content that carries C2PA metadata.

### Cited Findings
- **What YouTube requires disclosure for:** Realistic content that is fully or partly AI-generated or meaningfully altered. This covers content that makes a real person appear to say or do something they didn't, alters footage of a real event or place, or generates a realistic scene that didn't occur. Creators disclose it under "AI use" in YouTube Studio when uploading. — [YouTube Help 14328491](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491)
- **What YouTube exempts:** Non-realistic content, such as fantasy or fully animated videos. Also exempt are minor aesthetic edits, AI used for scripts, titles, thumbnails or brainstorming, and cloning your own voice for voiceovers. — [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491)
- **YouTube label placement:** Photorealistic AI content may get a label on the video player. Animated or non-photorealistic AI content may get the label in the expanded description. YouTube may auto-apply labels, for example from C2PA metadata or its own tools, and some cannot be changed. — [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491); [How this content was made](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15447836?hl=en)
- **YouTube penalties:** Consistent non-disclosure can lead to labels added by YouTube, content removal or suspension from the YouTube Partner Program. Disclosure itself does not limit reach or monetization. — [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491)
- **TikTok:**
  - Creators must label AIGC that depicts realistic-looking people or scenes.
  - TikTok auto-labels content made with its own AI tools or carrying C2PA Content Credentials, which it adopted in January 2025.
  - Ad rules are stricter: realistic AI ads must carry the label.
  - These details come from secondary sources, because I did not retrieve TikTok's own Help Center text. — [Hootsuite](https://blog.hootsuite.com/ai-disclosure-policy-for-tiktok/); [Fortune, 09/05/2024](https://fortune.com/2024/05/09/tiktok-ai-generated-content-label-misinformation/); [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/tiktok-officially-launches-new-in-stream-labels-for-ai-generated-content/694154/); [Echotik 2026](https://www.echotik.live/blog/tiktok-ai-content-labeling-rules-2026/)
- One cited 2025 study found that AI labels did not significantly change engagement. This comes from a secondary source and was not verified. — [Rewarx](https://www.rewarx.com/blogs/tiktok-ai-content-disclosure-rules-enforced)

### Inferences
- Photorealistic AI scenes of ancient Vietnam fall under "realistic scene that didn't occur" or "real people appearing to do things". This includes real figures such as Hai Bà Trưng, as well as realistic POV or CCTV-style footage. The safe default is to always disclose on both platforms and in Vietnam.
- A stylised or clearly animated look, such as 2D, painterly or diorama styles, is exempt on YouTube. It may also reduce accuracy backlash, because it does not claim to be real footage.
- The "CCTV of ancient times" format is deliberately made to look like real footage, so it clearly requires a label.

### Gaps
- TikTok's primary Help Center and Community Guidelines text for 2026 was not fetched.
- Whether TikTok's labels for Vietnam-region users appear in Vietnamese was not confirmed.

## 5. Data or credible commentary on optimal length and format for history Shorts and TikTok in Vietnam

### Takeaway
No credible Vietnam-specific data exists on the best length for history Shorts. Successful Vietnamese history channels use two main patterns:
- Under-60-second hero and anecdote clips, as on Dòng Máu Việt
- Long-form YouTube overviews of about 21 minutes, cut into short clips, as on EZ Sử

Marketing commentary suggests TikTok now favours videos over 30–60 seconds rather than ultra-short ones.

### Cited Findings
- Dòng Máu Việt built 2.5M TikTok followers and 750M+ total views with videos under 1 minute. EZ Sử's 21-minute long-form overview reached 13M views on YouTube, and its team reposts short clips to TikTok and Facebook. — [Tiền Phong, 03/09/2024](https://tienphong.vn/nhung-nguoi-tre-lam-clip-lich-su-trieu-view-post1668069.tpo)
- EZ Sử's timeline-under-video device lets viewers jump to specific periods. — [Đại Đoàn Kết, 09/09/2024](https://daidoanket.vn/ke-chuyen-lich-su-thoi-4-0-10289686.html)
- **Advertising Vietnam (03/2025):** Longer videos have larger average reach on TikTok, and going over 30 seconds is a reasonable compromise if over 1 minute is too costly. Stretching videos to 10 minutes does not automatically help. A cited figure says 22.2% of videos are 5–10 seconds long but average watch time is only about 3.1 seconds. — [Advertising Vietnam](https://advertisingvietnam.com/tiktok-co-dang-uu-tien-video-dai-hon-bat-mi-do-dai-toi-uu-de-tang-luot-xem-tren-tiktok-p26153)
- Globally, TikTok users now spend more time on longer videos than on ultra-short ones. — [The Star/AFP, 20/03/2025](https://www.thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2025/03/20/tiktok039s-ultra-short-videos-are-no-longer-the-biggest-draw-on-the-platform)
- **TikTok Vietnam's director:** Vietnamese users watch about 100 million hours of TikTok video per day. — [BBW, 06/2026](https://bbw.vn/nguoi-viet-nam-danh-moi-ngay-hon-100-trieu-gio-luot-tiktok-58364.html)
- **Posting times:** Marketing posts cite 19:00–21:00 (Thursday) as peak engagement, but this is not verified against TikTok data. — [Ecomobi](https://ecomobi.com/vi/khung-gio-dang-tiktok-bi-quyet-len-xu-huong-nhanh)
- **Story-driven history works on TikTok:** A non-AI clip about famous quotes of historical figures reached over 3M views in 3 days. This figure comes from a search-engine summary of the Dân trí article. — [Dân trí, 07/02/2023](https://dantri.com.vn/nhip-song-tre/nam-sinh-nganh-ky-thuat-ke-chuyen-lich-su-tren-tiktok-hut-hang-trieu-view-20230207194106718.htm)

### Inferences
- A practical pattern for Vietnamese history content:
  - TikTok and Facebook Reels: about 45–90 seconds, with a strong hook in the first 2–3 seconds.
  - YouTube Shorts: up to 60 seconds, or up to 3 minutes where allowed.
  - Episodic series ("Phần 1/2/3") for long-form stories, plus long-form YouTube compilations of the same series.
- Hooks rooted in anecdotes and quotes, such as famous lines or "did you know" facts, have a press-documented track record in Vietnam.

### Gaps
